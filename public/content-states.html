Rail content states

Everything the "While you were away" and "Up next" cards can show, and the rules behind them · benable-brand-prototype-v35

The logic

1The cards only exist on live-dashboard days. Days 1 and 3 are pre-campaign phases (Stay Tuned / Creators Found) with no rail at all, so their content starts at Day 4.
2In the prototype, every line is hand-written per demo day in pulseData.js → DAYS. Nothing is derived — each day carries a recap (a "since" anchor + 3–4 items) and an upNext list (2–3 items with ETAs). The tables below are that content, verbatim.
3The production intent: "While you were away" = the ranked deltas since the brand's last visit (what got handled for them, biggest first, max ~4); "Up next" = the nearest future events with honest ETAs (max ~3). The demo copy models the tone for both.
4Anatomy: recap items are bold fact + quiet tail ("4 packages shipped — first one already delivered"); up-next items are bold event + "— eta". Emoji leads every line.
5Copy rules (repo law): no zeros — a row shows a sentence about what's happening, never "0"; operational claims say "Katie's team"; no struggle updates — rematches read as reassurance ("3 stand-ins vetted", "Replacement matches found").
6Time voice, everywhere: the past gets real dates (Jul 16…), the present is "right now", and the future is always plain "up next" — per-step ETAs were removed from the timeline (Jul 27); precise ETAs live only in the "Up next" card, where they're a promise we track.
The data also carries a recap.closer per day (a CTA line like "New matches are waiting · Review matches") — the current Amine card design doesn't render it; it's kept in the data as the hook for a future notification/CTA slot.

Creator rows — the four anatomies

1Standard row — photo avatar, name + blue verified check, @handle · status line (see registers below) · 7 purple stage dashes filled through the current stage · chevron; clicking expands the dated stage-history timeline (dates → "right now" → "up next").
2Action row — a standard row whose status turns ⚑ orange because something waits on the brand (action on the creator, e.g. Day 16 Jade's reel approval). Action rows sort to the top of the table and count into the rail's amber badge + Needs-you filter.
3Sourcing row — hatched "?" avatar, name "Sourcing", subtitle "New creators for your campaign", shimmer status (rotating machine verbs, never a number), empty dashes; expands to the sourcing timeline (Brief studied → Scanning → Shortlisting → Your review).
4Matches-found row — the sourcing row after a rematch succeeds (found: true): blurred tease photo, "New matches found", "found this morning", ⚑ Review new matches; no expansion (review happens in the flow), sorts to top, feeds the rail badge.
Status motion registers (the status line is a LiveStatus, and every animation is a claim): shimmer = machine working right now (gradient sweep, rotating phrases) · katie = a human present (avatar + online dot + typing, never a spinner) · heartbeat = watching something live (one still sentence, gentle breathe) · celebrate = go-live (emoji bounces, words hold still) · facts/static = quiet truths, no motion. Emoji are stripped from status text at render — only celebrate keeps its one bouncing emoji.
